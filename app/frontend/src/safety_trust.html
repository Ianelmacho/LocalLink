The trust process
  1. 01

    Apply & verify

    Locals apply and go through identity and background checks to join the community.

  2. 02

    Connect & plan

    You chat, ask questions, and plan together with full visibility before anything is booked.

  3. 03

    Travel with confidence

    Clear plans, local guidance, and in-app support keep things smooth while you travel.

  4. 04

    Share & improve

    After the trip, both sides leave feedback to help keep the community strong.
